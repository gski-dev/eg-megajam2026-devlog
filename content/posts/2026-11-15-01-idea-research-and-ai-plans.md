## Give the idea a shape

We started with the idea of a high fantasy RTS, then had AI research the design and production needs. The result established Humans, Elves, and Goblins with shared class roles and distinct palettes, equipment, and architecture.

## Give AI a plan to work from

We turned those decisions into separate character, building, equipment, and animation plans. A generation brief could now specify a particular silhouette, faction palette, hairstyle, object, view, and downstream use.
