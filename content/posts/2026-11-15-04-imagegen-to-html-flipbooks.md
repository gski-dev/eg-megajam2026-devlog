## Ask for the action, frame by frame

We asked imagegen for twelve poses in a four-column, three-row sheet. Character identity and equipment references kept the design recognizable. A fixed camera, consistent ground baseline, and clear cell margins made the sheet playable as a flipbook.

## Give each pose its timing

The browser player crops the cells and uses saved keyframe timestamps to show anticipation, contact or release, follow-through, and recovery. Different pose intervals let us inspect the action's rhythm. Separate sheets cover contact, projectile flight and impact, or field behavior.
