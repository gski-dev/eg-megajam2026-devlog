## Describe one object from four sides

Imagegen produced individual front, left, back, and right references for characters, buildings, and equipment. Each set needed consistent proportions and details so Tripo had a coherent model to reconstruct.

## From references to a model

We used the four images in Tripo Studio's multiview workflow, then textured and exported the generated models. Humanoid characters used UE5 Mannequin rigging. Preparation and Unreal import followed the download.

The Tripo section includes our supplied screen recording alongside the source images.
