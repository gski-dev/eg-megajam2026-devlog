## See the descriptions together

We used imagegen to create concept sprite sheets from the plans and character descriptions. Putting the designs on shared sheets made it easier to compare silhouettes, equipment, and faction colors before moving on to separate model references.

The library includes character classes, outfits and gear, mounts and companions, and buildings for Humans, Elves, and Goblins.
